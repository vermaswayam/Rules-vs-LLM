# Changes for FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27

Apply all unchanged rules from rules_2024.md. Finance Act 2025 section 24 amends
section 115BAC(1A); section 20 amends section 87A, effective AY 2026-27.

- New regime slabs: 0-400,000 0%; 400,000-800,000 5%; 800,000-1,200,000 10%; 1,200,000-1,600,000 15%; 1,600,000-2,000,000 20%; 2,000,000-2,400,000 25%; above 2,400,000 30%.
- Resident new-regime section 87A rebate threshold becomes 1,200,000 and cap becomes 60,000. Above the threshold, marginal relief reduces tax to min(slab tax, income minus 1,200,000). Salary-only income is all slab-rate income, so the restriction excluding special-rate income is immaterial here.
- New standard deduction stays 75,000. Old standard deduction stays 50,000. Old slabs, old rebate, covered old deductions, surcharge rates, surcharge marginal relief, cess and rounding remain unchanged.
- Do not confuse FY 2025-26 with the later Income-tax Act 2025 tax year 2026-27. This project implements the Income-tax Act 1961 for the two years explicitly requested.
